# Growth Audit — AirBeamTV

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/airbeamtv-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic you already have, with a clickable prototype of the
Screen Mirroring Samsung TV onboarding and paywall rebuilt in AirBeamTV's own design. Prepared by Adapty, October 2026.

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Offer the 14,99 € year when they close | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Show all three prices before Apple's sheet | Paywall | CR +5–15% |
| 03 | Name their TV and what they'll watch | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 04 | Ask two questions and check their TV | What they watch · TV model · Loader | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Show your own proof before the price | Ready | CR +10–15% |

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not
measured lift for AirBeamTV.

**Grounding.** Every price, rating, quote and feature line comes from AirBeamTV's own App Store listing,
website (airbeam.tv) and in-app screens. Store prices are shown as published in euros; reviews are quoted
verbatim in their original language.

## Run locally

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8921
```

Then open http://localhost:8921/ — add `?open=proto` to land straight on the prototype.
